# recruiters.rip design system

Required for every product UI change. This document replaces the previous Warm Bone, flat-spectrum, mint/rose status system. It governs the companion and the account/setup website; their source repositories remain independently buildable.

## Source and precedence

- Approved artifact: [recruiters.rip design system](https://claude.ai/artifact/8PZTo24Gv7onffw9MngADm), retrieved 15 October 2026.
- Downloaded source: the current author-written `README.md`, `tokens.json`, component bundle and previews, seven font files and current `assets/Logos` files. The complete download also contains generated component API cards.
- The artifact's generated `tokens.css`, `manifest.json` and some generated API descriptions still contain the previous design. Do not copy those stale values. Current `tokens.json` and the author-written brand rules take precedence. The sample bundle repeats azure at the end of its dispersion declaration; use violet as the fifth stop, as specified by the current palette and brand rules.
- Runtime tokens live in `ui/design-tokens.css` here and `src/app/design-tokens.css` in the website. Their values must agree. Fonts and logos are local assets, not remote dependencies.
- The supplied bundle targets a React global. The website adapts the supplied components to its existing React implementation; the companion uses equivalent semantic HTML. Neither app loads a second framework or imports its sibling at build/runtime.

## Visual principle

**Dark ground, clear surfaces, light at the edge.** Structure comes from fine rules, not rounded tiles, coloured panels or drop shadows. All text ranges left. Colour appears only where a thin edge catches light. This is not frosted-card styling: do not add generic backdrop blur, gradient fields, opaque coloured bands or decorative animation.

Three voices have distinct jobs:

- **Instrument Serif:** upright headlines, section headings and panel titles, never smaller than 28px.
- **Geist Mono:** navigation, buttons, labels, metadata, code and numbers.
- **Geist:** reading copy and field content.

No italics, emoji, decorative serial numbers, centred headlines or chromatic body/link/status text. Keep the brand lowercase: **recruiters.rip**. Product capabilities, privacy claims and status labels must remain true; the artifact's examples are not evidence of features such as an employer posting flow. Warm introductions are real but live only in the connected agent (`career_intro_paths`); the companion has no introductions screen, so never draw one.

### Dispersion

Five stops, in this exact order, blended in OKLab:

| Token | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `color-disperse-red` | `oklch(0.76 0.15 15)` |
| `color-disperse-amber` | `oklch(0.85 0.13 70)` |
| `color-disperse-green` | `oklch(0.86 0.15 150)` |
| `color-disperse-azure` | `oklch(0.80 0.12 250)` |
| `color-disperse-violet` | `oklch(0.78 0.12 295)` |

Use `--rr-dispersion` horizontally and `--rr-dispersion-v` vertically. They are allowed on 1px edges, rules, active-navigation underlines, hover borders and the architectural mark's rim. They are never large-area fills, discrete colour bands, single-hue states or coloured logo fills. A focused input may use a 2px dispersion rule. Azure at 35–45% is allowed for selection and focused-input/lit-edge halos. On a dark ground, a lit rule can emit a static blurred copy of itself; it does not emit on the day theme.

A headline may emphasize at most one word with upright red/azure fringes. The word remains ink, not gradient-filled text. Dispersion is decoration; focus and selection must also have a clear monochrome/text/ARIA signal.

## Layout and shape

- 8px base scale: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 64, 80 and 120px.
- Content maximum: 1200px. The content container is centred; text inside it is not.
- Section gap: 80px. Related blocks: 40px. Sibling gap: 16px. Card padding and ordinary grid gutters: 24px.
- Feature entries form a ruled grid without gaps. Do not confuse a CSS page layout with a data grid; actual tabular grids/heatmaps use AG Grid.
- Cards, panels, inputs, dialogs and ledgers: square. Buttons and tags: 2px radius. Circular radii are reserved for genuine status dots.
- Hairlines are 1px. Strong ink rules mark the beginning of a ledger or field. Low-contrast facet rules are structural decoration, not the only boundary identifying an interactive control.
- No lifted-card shadows. The current shadow tokens permit keyboard focus, input halos and dark-only edge glow; `shadow-light-glow` is reserved for a splash and is not a general surface effect.
- Nothing animates. No moving light, hover transforms, pulsing status, shimmer, smooth-scroll animation or layout transitions.

## Shared components and states

### Brand

Use the supplied mirrored Instrument Serif **r** paths. The two feet meet at one point; no extra plinth, slit, line, frame or colour is added. Use current `recruiters-rip-mark.svg`/`-reversed.svg` and lockup assets. Do not redraw the mark using another font. Keep clear space of half the mark's height, minimum mark height 12px; use the supplied favicon cut below 24px. The application icon is the light mark on void. A large architectural mark can have an 8% body and a thin dispersion rim on a dark hero, behind and clear of the content.

### Actions

One primary task per action group: ink fill and canvas text. Secondary buttons are transparent with an ink outline. Low-emphasis actions are text buttons. Mono labels, 44px minimum control height, wrapping rather than clipping at narrow widths. Hover lights the edge, not the whole control. Disabled controls have dust text, no filled background, no hover lighting and `cursor: not-allowed`.

**Tabs are navigation; buttons are actions.** `.tabs > .tab[role=tab]` are ruled mono text: the selected tab carries an ink underline plus the dispersion rule, the same language as the top bar, and `aria-selected` marks it; tabs are never boxed. Boxed `primary`/`ghost`/`text` buttons are reserved for actions. This applies to the Me categories (CVs / Facts / Preferences / Career), the Jobs tabs (Jobs / Reviewed / Applications / Blocked) and Settings (General / Backups).

Keyboard focus is a 2px canvas gap followed by a solid 2px ink ring. Never replace it with colour alone. Keep focus visible inside clipped/scrolling containers. Links remain ink; a thin dispersion underline may indicate interaction.

**Destructive exception (user-approved):** **Delete CV** uses the existing `color-disperse-red` token for its thin border and a distinct `.destructive` class. This deliberately overrides the no-single-hue-control rule for this action only. Keep neutral text, transparent fill, the explicit Delete CV label, normal disabled treatment and the monochrome keyboard-focus ring; never add a red fill or depend on colour alone.

### Fields

A visible label, transparent field and bottom ink rule, not a rounded filled box. Preserve semantic input types, password masking, bounds and validation. Focus uses the two-pixel dispersion rule plus an azure halo and a visible high-contrast keyboard indicator. Native selects keep usable options. Errors have explicit text and `role="alert"`; colour is not an error message.

### Lists, readers and status

Career records are a master/detail list, not a custom table engine. Keep full-result ordering before pagination and readable narrow-screen navigation. Current tabs use `aria-selected`, current records `aria-current`, with text weight and a thin edge or rule, never a mint panel. Do not change data or ranking semantics to match a mockup.

State labels come from actual account, membership, device, lock or request state. Retain loading, empty, error, partial, stale, cancelled and disabled states. Do not infer membership, successful pairing, model completion or permission from the visual design. Notifications remain readable and announced with appropriate status/alert semantics. In the companion the notice (`#notice`) is one top-right toast below the topbar: surface fill, facet rule, one ink left edge, no colour or glow, never a banner that pushes content. Confirmations (copied, saved, allowed) clear themselves after five seconds; errors and in-progress instructions (`Complete sign-in … then return here`) stay until something replaces them.

## Screen designs

### Website

- The website uses dark/void surfaces throughout, the reversed logo and ruled Home / Pricing / Help / Downloads / Account navigation. Active routes use one ink-outline frame and semibold text, not stacked underlines. The landing page groups its product pitch and compact price with one **View pricing** action above an ordered feature walkthrough. The public Pricing page owns monthly/yearly billing selection and purchase. The independently owned frontend records its complete website screen rules in its own `design.md`.
- Public Help is a task-led reference: install Companion, connect account, import a CV, connect Codex or Claude, review jobs, then open application documents. Browser capture and form filling come later and are optional. Use readable prose, exact controls, short steps, concrete prompts and an index; avoid marketing panels, repeated disclaimers and setup subtitles. Preserve `#install`, `#connect`, `#applications` and `#extension` deep links. Feature guides and sourced competitor comparisons are linked from the website.
- Downloads begins with a computer choice and one selected installer, with short OS-specific steps and collapsed file details. Plugins installed from Companion are not download rows. Mac, Windows and Linux instructions must remain distinct from actual published file availability; never imply an unpublished installer or browser release exists.
- Authentication, billing, pairing and signed-device boundaries belong to the website and middleware; loading, pending and error states never imply completion. Career data and provider keys stay off the website. Final reasoning belongs to the user's AI app; optional approved API usage remains separately billed.

### Companion

- Dark/void workspace by default. Compact ruled top navigation with the supplied reversed lockup.
- Navigation is **Me / Jobs / Settings / Help**; Me opens by default. Me keeps the **CVs / Facts / Preferences / Career** browser and its action row: **Import CV**, **Jobs**, and **Connect account** while unpaired when not already the setup action. No generic files-folder or duplicate AI apps action appears in Me. Backup and restore belong together in **Settings → Backups**. An incomplete setup adds one compact ruled next-action section above the action row, never a blocking wizard: connect account → import CV → connect Codex or Claude. The primary AI setup action opens **Settings → General → AI apps**; the real AI apps section remains there. Account connection is recommended for new jobs, not required to import a CV or use saved jobs and applications. All workspace tabs remain usable in partial setup. Locked saved work offers **Unlock**.
- Setup state is authoritative: `paired` is the stored account pairing, not membership; `hasCv` is true only for a non-archived `career.sources` entry with `purpose: 'cv'` and non-empty imported text, never a parsed profile or an import click; connected AI means a live recognized Codex/Claude client in `mcpClients`. Host availability/configuration is not a connection. When all three facts are present, the setup section disappears and the ordinary workspace remains, with **Jobs** as the next navigation action. If a conversation closes, the small section explains how to reconnect without claiming data was lost. There is no completion flag, dismissal persistence or mandatory order. Setup rendering is keyed by these facts, and ordinary state polling does not reload lists, change tabs or reset job selection. An unavailable local state read gives **Retry**, keeps already displayed records and labels connection status unavailable rather than clearing the workspace.
- **Preferences** is the visible read-only record of what matching uses: four rows — Direction, Location, Employment, Work. Direction shows target roles, accepted alternatives, avoided roles and the saved direction notes; Location shows places, work modes, relocation, travel and availability; Employment shows types, minimum salary and sponsorship; Work shows responsibility mode, direct reports, review emphasis, technologies, domains and company context. Unknown values say `Not saved`; nothing is inferred from the CV. The connected agent writes these fields through `career_preferences_update`; `acceptedAlternatives` and `excludedRoles` are dedicated fields, not buried in notes. **Career** lists the work history the agent extracted from current CVs and confidently matched to JobStash: one plain row per role (employer as the title; mono summary `Title` then `Mar 2021 – Jan 2024`, `Present` for a current role, `Not stated` for a missing date), newest first, identical roles shown once. Its reader shows Employment (Company, Title, Dates), Inferred from CV (the CV name, plus `CV changed since extraction` when the CV hash moved) and one secondary **Go to {company}’s profile** button; the companion resolves the saved entity ref through the signed relay to the ecosystem.vision page key (`organizations/names/<normalizedName>` or `projects/names/<normalizedName>`) and opens it in the default browser, never a guessed name URL. An employer without an unambiguous page reports `{company} has no public ecosystem.vision page yet.` in the list status. Unmatched employers never appear; the empty state says the agent extracts and links them.
- A selected CV exposes **Delete CV** in its top action row, before metadata and document text, not after the document. Do not append an explanatory deletion paragraph. The native confirmation retains **Cancel / Delete CV**, with Cancel focused; deletion still removes linked remote copies before committing local removal.
- **Jobs and Applications lists have no Open actions.** Selecting a record shows one **Job and documents** group after its coherent company/publication/role metadata (after Application metadata in Applications). **Open job**, **Open CV** and **Open letter** appear once each when available, all in the same secondary outlined style and wrapping action columns. Each document's saved date or missing/unavailable status belongs within its own column beneath the button, never between unrelated buttons. Omit raw export counts and duplicate draft-date metadata; retain independent application/interview information. Without a job URL, the group is **Documents**. Saved dates describe historical artifacts, not proof that the latest draft or career context is newly ready. Ambiguous matches direct the user to Applications, never pick by company/title. `career-open-document` accepts only record kind/ID, document kind and an opaque selection token; main resolves the saved export URI through private-file validation and rechecks identity/files before native shell opening. `career-open-job` accepts kind/ID and resolves either the saved job ID or the exact application's current job URL, never a renderer URL. No opening action starts generation. Use the existing shared notice for success/errors; never overwrite list counts or pagination status. Background refresh preserves the selected reader, page, action focus and scroll position.
- List projection/refresh never resolves or hashes generated PDF files and returns no document-selection tokens. The selected job or application uses `career-read` for document availability; background refresh re-reads that selected detail even when the list row is unchanged, so changed exports update actions without moving selection or focus. Actual opening repeats the private-file and identity checks.
- Jobs holds the job dates, reviews, applications and the block list. Help follows connect account → import CV → Get Codex / Get Claude if needed → Set up → Jobs / Continue in Codex or Claude. It says to send the message in the app or press Enter in the text window that opens. The exact starting prompt (`Use the recruiters.rip skill to find me a job.`) remains in a selectable `pre.command`; instructions/download URLs and the running package version remain. Saved CVs and jobs remain readable without internet or an account. Never present a copied prompt or opened app as an executing model.
- Customer-facing setup uses ordinary task words: jobs, saved work, your app, your account and this computer. Do not expose catalogue, captured/local records, host, pairing, source or MCP terminology in normal instructions. Keep exact browser menu labels where users must find them. Developer details and raw app-discovery diagnostics belong under **Advanced**, never in the main recovery explanation or the default Linux setup route. Optional **Form filling** opens with **Optional · help fill forms**; explain the separately billed OpenAI API key only inside that disclosure.
- Jobs is a dedicated full-height tab: a tab strip, the slider row, then the list and reader. The strip (`.tabs > .tab[role=tab]`, each with its live count from `career-list` `counts`) is **Jobs N · Reviewed N · Applications N · Blocked N**; tabs follow the navigation rule above (ruled mono text, selected tab underlined, never boxed). The list head holds only the range `1–25 of 1,342`; empty states name the tab's cause (no jobs in the window / nothing reviewed yet).
- Pinned pagination is a CSS contract, independent of the renderer: `main` (flex 1, `min-height: 0`) → `#settings` (`height: 100%`) → `.career-view` (`height: 100%`, grid) → `.career-browser` (grid `auto minmax(0, 1fr)`; `#career-browser` adds a row for the rail: `auto auto minmax(0, 1fr)`) → `.career-panes` (grid item, stretched, `min-height: 0`; never `height: 100%`, which would measure the browser including its toolbar) → `.career-list` (grid `auto auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto`, `overflow: hidden`). Only `(max-width: 479px)` or `(max-height: 639px)` switch the column to natural height and page scrolling.
- One vocabulary, everywhere: **AI review** is the user's own Codex/Claude reading the full adverts, which yields **Fit** and Recommended / Needs clarification / Not recommended. Fit is shown because it is the agent's verdict. No priority, rank, score or send wording appears in the interface. Nothing in the companion decides which jobs the agent sees except the window and the block list.
- Card anatomy (`li.career-card`), left to right: (1) The record button: a 36px company mark (`.career-card-mark`, facet rule, surface fill, the company's initial in mono; the organization's https logo drawn over it when the catalogue supplies `logoUrl`, removed on load error) beside three plain lines with 20px vertical padding and 8px gaps — role title (Geist 15/500); `company · Published 17 Oct 2026` (company ink-2, date ink); one Geist Mono 12px smoke line of facts joined by ` · ` (seniority, salary, city or location · work mode, commitment, classification), ellipsised to a single line, missing values omitted. No chips, boxes, uppercase or colour fills. (2) The 112px right column (`.career-card-side`), a sibling of the button, never nested inside it: a reviewed job shows the overall fit as a 24px tabular Geist Mono figure (`.career-card-figure`) captioned `Fit` (`Fit · out of date` when stale; no decision text, no dimensions); an **ineligible** job (a review whose location match is `Location incompatible` or `Excluded by a gate`, or an established incompatibility from a stale review) shows that verdict word alone — a 12px ink caption (`.career-card-verdict`) wrapping within the column if it must — never the number; its aria-label says `not eligible, {label}`; an open (unreviewed) job shows nothing on the right. Logos are the one exception to the renderer's no-network rule: the page CSP allows `img-src https:` and `main.mjs` lets through `image` requests to https hosts only, with `referrerpolicy=no-referrer` and the in-memory session (no cookies); scripts, styles, fetches, frames and navigation stay local. Jobs is chronological; Reviewed is fit, ineligible last. **Job reader** (`#career-reader`): a candidate must see in one screen what the job is, where it stands and why, then the evidence. (4) **Checks**: one ledger of everything that can keep the job out — Location, Relocation, Salary (`pay · Below your minimum`), the three gates as Passes / Needs confirmation / Fails. (5) **Fit** chart (heading `Fit excluding location — not eligible` / `Fit — excluded by a gate` for ineligible reviews).
