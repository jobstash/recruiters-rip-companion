Data and deletion
Delete rankings clears saved rankings and the cached job list. Your profile, preferences, source CVs, career facts and application drafts stay. Exported files are not deleted.
Local career data is encrypted. Exported PDFs, lists and backups are ordinary files. Content read by your chat app is shared with its model provider.




Chat-app access

Local updates


Blocking updates prevents chat apps from changing local data. Saved records remain readable. This does not stop Codex or Claude replies, reasoning, or browser actions.




Connection recovery

Repair if your chat app can no longer reach Recruiters.rip. Reset only to switch accounts or replace a revoked device: your current local data is archived, never shown to the next account. Revoke the old device in your account settings separately.
