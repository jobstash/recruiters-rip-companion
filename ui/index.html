            Set up and review jobs
            

              

            1. In Settings, choose Connect account. Sign in in the browser, choose Connect account → Open Companion, then approve the named account back here.
              2. 

            2. In Me, choose Import CV and select a PDF, Word document (.docx) or text file.
              3. 

            3. In Settings → AI apps, choose Get Codex or Get Claude if you need to install one. Sign in there, then choose Set up in Companion.
              4. 

            4. Restart that app so it loads recruiters.rip: quit Claude completely and reopen it, restart Codex, or start a new Claude Code session.
              5. 

            5. Open Jobs and choose Continue in Codex or Continue in Claude. Send the message in that app, or press Enter in the text window that opens. Its reviews appear under Reviewed.
              6. 

            6. In Settings → AI apps, choose Copy prompt and paste it into Codex, Claude Code or another agent. It adds recruiters.rip for you. Then restart that app or start a new session.
              7. 

            7. Open Jobs and choose Copy prompt, paste the message into your AI app and send it. Its reviews appear under Reviewed.
              8. 

            You don’t need to choose Set up again after an update. Each time Companion starts, it updates what Codex and Claude know about recruiters.rip. A conversation that’s already open may keep the old instructions, so start a new one.
            

            

            

            In your AI app
            

            Keep Companion open. To start from a conversation you already have, send:
            Use recruiters.rip to find jobs that fit my CV. Ask about any missing preferences and explain which roles fit and why.
            New jobs need a recruiters.rip subscription. Codex or Claude runs on your own account, and its provider bills you for any plan it needs. You don’t need the browser extension or an API key to review jobs.
            My AI app can’t find recruiters.rip
            Choose Set up in Settings → AI apps, then quit the app completely and reopen it (in Claude Code, start a new session). Turn on recruiters.rip if the app asks. If Continue in Codex or Continue in Claude doesn’t open the app, copy the message into it yourself.
            Check that the Companion container is running, and that the docker command works where your AI app runs. Then paste the setup prompt from Settings → AI apps into the app again and start a new session.
            

            

            

            Open your CV and letter
            

            1. Ask your AI to create and save a tailored CV and cover letter for a job.
            2. Open Jobs in Companion and select that job.
            3. Under Job and documents, choose Open CV or Open letter. Check them, then choose Open job to apply.
            You can open the same documents from the application in Pipeline. Both PDFs are saved in one folder named after the company and job title, as Your name - CV.pdf and Your name - COVER LETTER.pdf. If they’re missing, ask your AI to create and save them.
            

            

            

            Apply from your browser
            

            Add the extension in Settings → Browser extensions, then keep Companion open.
            1. Open a job ad or application form and choose the recruiters.rip toolbar icon. It reads the page and gets any saved answers ready.
            2. Choose Prepare application. Codex or Claude opens with a request for your CV, introduction and answers. Click Send there, or press Enter in the text window.The extension copies a request for your CV, introduction and answers. Paste it into Codex or Claude and send it. To switch AI app, use Options in the extension.
            3. When it is ready, click an empty answer box and press ⌘V on Mac or Ctrl+V on Windows and Linux. Show CV shows the file to upload; Copy intro copies your introduction for a cover letter or message box.
            4. Check the form, then submit it yourself.
            Saved answers work without an API key. If you’ve already prepared a job, the extension reuses that work.
            The extension cannot prepare this page
            • No job found on this page: open the job ad or application form itself, then choose Read page.
            • Choose the job you want to apply for: select a job. It opens in a new tab; choose the toolbar icon there.
            • Companion is not open: open it, then choose Retry. Not installed? Choose Get Companion.
            • Locked: unlock your saved data in Companion, then read the page again.
            • Update Companion: install the update in Settings → Updates, then read the page again.
            • Import your CV: in Me, choose Import CV.
            • Codex or Claude is unavailable: choose the other app under Options, or set it up in Settings → AI apps.
            • Extra form help is unavailable: choose Use saved answers.
            • The job or your saved details changed: choose Refresh answers or Update application.
            

            

            

            Backups
            

            In Settings → Backups, choose Save backup to keep a private copy of your work. To recover one, choose Restore backup, select the file, check the preview and choose Restore profile.
            Restoring replaces your current profile. Your sign-in and API keys stay as they are. Companion saves the previous profile first, and you’ll find it in Open backups. Backup files have no password.
            Delete a CV
            Open Me → CVs, select the CV, choose Delete CV and confirm. The CV is removed from your profile on this computer. Your other CVs, applications and backups stay as they are.
