Your local career data
Source CVs, profile facts and application drafts are encrypted locally. Content read by your chat app is shared with its model provider, not uploaded to Recruiters.rip.
Exported PDFs and backups are ordinary local files. Choose a private location when sharing or copying them.


Connection recovery
Reset only to switch platform accounts or replace a revoked device. Your current local career data is archived, not exposed to the next account. Revoke the old device in your platform account separately.
